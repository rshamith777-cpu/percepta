## Slide 1: Title & Vision
* **Title:** **KRIYA-OS**
* **Subtitle:** The World's First "Zero-UI, Zero-CAD" Multimodal Field Engineer Copilot
* **Founding Vision:** Transforming any ₹10,000 Android smartphone into a real-time vernacular master engineer for industrial, solar, and EV infrastructure maintenance.
* **Powered 100% by:** Google DeepMind & Google Cloud Ecosystem.

---

## Slide 2: The Physical-World Crisis (Problem)
1. **The 29.5 Million Skilled Technician Deficit:** Rapid industrialization across India (Solar Grids, EV Charging, Manufacturing, Telecom, Railways) is crippled by a massive technical talent shortage.
2. **The High Cost of Equipment Downtime:** Unplanned machine outages cost enterprises **₹80,000 to ₹15,00,000 per incident** while waiting 48–72 hours for senior OEM engineers.
3. **The Failure of 1,500-Page English PDF Manuals:** Field technicians in greasy, dark electrical cabinets cannot read 1,500-page English technical documentation.
4. **The Economic Failure of Legacy AR:** Traditional industrial AR headsets cost **$3,500+** per device and demand **4–8 weeks of expensive 3D CAD modeling** per machine model.

---

## Slide 3: The "Zero-UI, Zero-CAD" Breakthrough (Solution)
* **Point Camera:** Technician opens KriyaOS on an entry-level smartphone camera facing the cabinet.
* **Speak Regional Language:** Asks questions naturally in **Hinglish, Hindi, Tamil, Marathi, or English** via **Gemini Live**.
* **Instant AR Visual Overlay:** **Nano Banana** dynamically renders glowing neon bounding boxes, directional arrows, and step badges directly onto the live hardware photo in `<3 seconds` without 3D models.
* **Eyes-Free Acoustic Telemetry:** **Lyria** spatial harmonic audio guides technician hands without staring at the screen.
* **Offline Life-Safety Watchdog:** On-device **Gemma** guards worker safety locally in `<28ms` without internet dependency.
* **Autonomous Enterprise Closure:** **Google Agent Skills** deduct spare parts from inventory, issue purchase orders, and sign off compliance.
