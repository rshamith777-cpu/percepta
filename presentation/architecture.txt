================================================================
Key Principles (displayed alongside diagram):
- Deterministic gate: BLOCK is absolute, never overridable
- Advisory only: AI output never certifies safe-to-touch
- Truthful status: no green "connected" from config alone
- Offline-first: localStorage, explicit sync only
- No background operations: reconnect does not auto-sync
- Synthetic equipment: all demo confirmed de-energized
